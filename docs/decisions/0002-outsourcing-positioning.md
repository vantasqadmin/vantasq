# 0002. Position as an offshore outsourcing partner

Status: Superseded by [0014](0014-no-team-location.md)
Date: 2026-10-01

## Context

"Digital agency offering many services" is a crowded pitch. What the business
actually sells is dedicated Philippine specialists who work the client's
hours, recruited and managed by Vantasq.

## Decision

Every page speaks in the outsourcing voice. The services are grouped into five
roles, each with its own page (`digital-marketing-seo`, `web-design`,
`video-editing`, `virtual-assistant-services`, `ai-automation`), and the copy
sells the people doing the work rather than the agency as a black box.

## Consequences

- New pages and copy edits follow this voice, including the portfolio.
- Titles, meta descriptions and JSON-LD carry the outsourcing keywords, so
  copy changes must keep them in step.
- Pricing questions follow naturally from this pitch; see
  [0003](0003-no-prices.md) for why the site does not answer them.
