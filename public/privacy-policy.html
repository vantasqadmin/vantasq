This Privacy Policy applies to Vantasq and describes how we handle information collected through our website and services. By using our website or engaging our services, you agree to the terms described herein.

01 Information We Collect

This is a brochure website. It has no accounts and no checkout. Apart from Google Analytics, described below, it has no trackers. What else we collect depends on how you get in touch with us.

Information You Give Us

  • Contact details — your name, email address, and phone number (optional) when you write to us. The form on our site opens a pre-filled message in your own email app; nothing is submitted to this website itself.
  • Project details — what you tell us about your business and goals, and any materials you share with us while we work together.
  • Correspondence — emails and messages you send us, including through Facebook or Instagram.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Google Analytics — we use Google Analytics 4 to see how the site is used: which pages are viewed, how visitors arrive, the device and browser type, and an approximate (city-level) location derived from your IP address, which Google does not store. This is reported to us only in aggregate; we do not use it to identify you. See Section 4 for the cookies involved and how to opt out.
  • Server logs — our hosting provider, Cloudflare, records your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested in order to serve the site and protect it from abuse. We do not use this to identify or profile you.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics — Cloudflare also counts page views for us with a small script that sets no cookies and does not identify or fingerprint visitors.

02 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To provide and deliver the services you engage us for.
  • To send marketing communications — only if you have asked for them. You may opt out at any time.
  • To understand how the website is used, through Google Analytics, so we can improve it.
  • To keep the website secure and available.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We will never sell your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

03 Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

Service Providers

A small number of providers handle data on our behalf: Cloudflare hosts this website, Google provides our email and our analytics (Google Analytics), and, for clients, the platforms we use to deliver your project (such as advertising or social media accounts) process the data you ask us to manage there. These providers may only use your information to provide their service to us.

Business Transfers

If Vantasq is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental authority, or where we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, or investigate fraud.

04 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies for one purpose: to measure how the site is used. We do not use advertising or marketing cookies.

Cookies on This Site

  • Google Analytics — _ga and _ga_* distinguish one visitor from another so that page views can be counted as visits. They hold a random identifier, not your name or email, and last up to two years.
  • Cloudflare — our hosting provider may set a strictly necessary cookie to tell real visitors apart from automated traffic. It contains no personal information and expires after 30 minutes.

Opting Out

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site works the same without them. To stop Google Analytics specifically, install Google's browser opt-out add-on. How Google handles the data it collects is described in Google's privacy & terms.

The site does not store anything in your browser's local storage.

05 Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

  • Client project data — retained for the duration of the engagement and up to 3 years after project completion for record-keeping.
  • Enquiries — retained for up to 12 months unless we go on to work together.
  • Analytics data — Google Analytics keeps visitor-level data for 14 months; the aggregate reports we see are kept indefinitely.
  • Marketing data — retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion.

When your data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

06 Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

  • All traffic to this website is encrypted (HTTPS), and browsers are instructed to always use it.
  • Your information lives in our email and business accounts, which are protected with strong passwords and two-factor authentication.
  • Only the people working on your project have access to it.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

07 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction — request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion — request that we delete your personal information ("right to be forgotten"), subject to legal obligations.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Objection — object to processing of your data for direct marketing or based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw Consent — withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on your consent.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@vantasq.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

08 Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, social media platforms (such as Facebook and Instagram), and other external services. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those third-party sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external sites you visit.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites.

09 Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete that information.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or services. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
  • Notify active clients via email where required.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of our website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

11 Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please reach out to us:

  • Email: info@vantasq.com
  • Facebook: Vantasq
  • Response time: Within 24 hours, Monday to Saturday, 8AM – 8PM

Your privacy matters to us. We are committed to handling your information responsibly and transparently at every step.
