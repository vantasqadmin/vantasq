FAQ

Questions,answered.

The things people ask before they outsource anything. Anything else, just ask.

How quickly can someone start?

Most roles are matched and onboarded within 3–5 business days of sign-off. We reply to every enquiry within 24 hours, Monday to Saturday.

Do I manage the person day to day?

You set the priorities and review the output. We handle recruitment, supervision, quality checks and cover, so you are not running an offshore HR function.

What hours does my outsourced team work?

Yours. Your specialists work your business hours and overlap with your day, whether you are in the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia or anywhere else.

What happens if my specialist is sick or leaves?

We cover the seat. Every account has a backup briefed on the work, and finding a replacement is our job, not yours.

Is my data safe with an offshore team?

Accounts, files and data stay in your own systems. Access is role-based and revocable, and everyone working on your account signs an NDA.

Can I outsource more than one role?

Yes, and most clients do. One partner covers your website, marketing, video and admin, so nothing falls between the cracks.
